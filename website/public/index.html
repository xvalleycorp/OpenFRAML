Fraud and AML protection without the licence bill.

OpenFRAML stops fraudulent payments before they leave the bank and covers transaction monitoring, watchlist screening, case management and regulatory reporting. Open source, on your own servers, under rules your team controls.

No licence fees · Apache 2.0 · on-premise on commodity x86, no GPU

Why OpenFRAML

Lower cost. Full control.

Cost

Pay for servers, not licences

  • No licence fee

    Not per transaction, account, module or year. Your volume grows; the bill does not.

  • Ordinary hardware

    Commodity x86 servers, no GPU, no appliance. Sizing comes from a public benchmark, not from a quote.

  • Customers clear their own doubts

    CHALLENGE asks the customer to confirm a doubtful payment instead of putting it in an analyst’s queue.

  • Compliance work, shared

    Thresholds, lists and report templates live in a country pack, maintained once and used by every bank in the country.

Control

The bank owns the platform

  • Your rules

    Your team writes and changes the decision tables. No change request to a vendor, no waiting for a release.

  • Your data

    Runs on-premise; customer data never leaves the bank. Audit records hold no personal data.

  • Your code

    Apache 2.0: read, audit, change or fork every line. No lock-in, no exit project.

  • Your explanations

    Every decision records the rule version, model version and reason codes, and can be replayed for an auditor or the regulator. Models never override mandatory rules.

Instant payments cannot be recalled. The gate is the only moment to stop one.

The Payment Gate decides after the customer confirms a payment and before the payment hub sends it to the switch. Everything it reads is precomputed and held in memory; nothing in the hot path queries a database.

  • ALLOW

    Let the payment through

    Send it to the switch.

  • CHALLENGE

    Ask for more

    Show a scam warning, re-authenticate, or have the customer confirm again.

  • HOLD

    Keep it back for a quick review

    Open a high-priority case and contact the customer.

  • DENY

    Refuse it

    Tell the customer, and report if required.

Two paths, one system

Inline · synchronous

Decide in the payment flow

A few tens of milliseconds, inside the payment flow. Watchlist first, then profiles, velocity, the model with a time budget, and the decision table. Served over gRPC; the caller’s deadline is the gate’s time budget.

Payment Gate · Gate Service · phase 1

Asynchronous · volume

Monitor, screen, investigate, report

AML and fraud scenarios after the transaction, screening against sanctions, PEP and national lists, alerts with four-eyes approval, and reports in each country’s templates.

Monitoring · Screening · Cases · Reporting · phase 2

Benchmark

Measured in the open

A JMH benchmark and an open-loop gRPC load test run on a synthetic bank of 100,000 customers with the Vietnam pack’s payment mix. The open loop measures each payment from the moment it was due, so a slow server shows in the percentiles.

First runs on one 8-core development machine, load generator included, 25 October 2026. The 30 ms target is not verified yet: the next runs are on Linux x86 servers with Generational ZGC.

Country packs

A country-neutral core. Vietnam first.

Legal thresholds, report templates, lists, schemes, time zone and languages live in a versioned country pack. The core is tested against an invented country, so nothing national leaks into it.

Get started

Requires JDK 21, Maven 3.9 or newer, and git. RuleStone is not on Maven Central yet, so the script installs it from source at a pinned commit.

git clone https://github.com/xvalleycorp/OpenFRAML
cd OpenFRAML
./scripts/install-rulestone.sh
mvn verify
Gate gate = Gate.builder(CountryPack.forCountry("VN"))
        .profiles(profileStore)
        .velocity(new InMemoryVelocityStore())
        .watchlist(watchlist)
        .recordSink(kafkaPublisher)
        .build();

GateOutcome outcome = gate.decide(event, Duration.ofMillis(30));
// CHALLENGE · [ACTIVE_CALL_NEW_BENEFICIARY, ...]
